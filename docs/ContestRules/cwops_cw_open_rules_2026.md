# CW Open 2026 Rules (condensed)

## Overview

- **Sponsor:** The CW Operators' Club (CWops)
- **Date:** September 5, 2026 (first Saturday in September)
- **Mode:** CW only
- **Bands:** 160, 80, 40, 20, 15 and 10 meters
- **Eligibility:** All amateurs are encouraged to participate.
- **Contact:** Bruce, N1LN, for CWO Manager inquiries; cwo@cwops.org for log or other problems.

## Object

Work as many CW stations worldwide as possible within each session. Each station may be worked once per band in each session.

## Sessions

| Session | Date | Time (UTC) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | September 5, 2026 | 0000 - 0359z |
| 2 | September 5, 2026 | 1200 - 1559z |
| 3 | September 5, 2026 | 2000 - 2359z |

- Entry may be made to any or all sessions. Each session is scored separately.
- There are no time-off periods.

## Exchange

- Sequential serial number and name.
- Serial numbers start at 001 for each session and do not accumulate across sessions.

## Scoring

- **QSO points:** One point per valid two-way QSO with correct exchange. You may claim points for a station only once per band per session. Do not remove dupes from your log; duplicates score zero points with no penalty.
- **Multipliers:** Each different call sign is a multiplier the first time it is worked in each session. Working the same call on another band adds QSO points but not a multiplier. A busted call is not a multiplier, and if one is found during log checking, that multiplier is removed. (The page names no other multiplier; zones, prefixes and countries are never mentioned.)
- **Session score:** Total QSO points x total multipliers.
- **Combined score:** If multiple sessions are entered, the sum of session scores is reported separately.

## Station Classes

- **Single operator only.** No assistance from another operator, locally or remotely, except Packet.
- **Power levels:** QRP 5 W or less; Low 100 W or less; High any legal power above 100 W.
- Only one transmitted signal is allowed at any time.
- Packet, skimmer and reverse beacon networks are allowed. Packet must be a cluster arrangement that spots stations for others as well as yourself. Entering as "SO Assisted" or any multi-operator category is taken to mean single operator with Packet.
- Self-spotting is allowed.
- There are currently no multi-operator categories.

## Team Competition

1. A team has two or more single operators, with up to ten operators per team.
2. Teams may compete against each other with no geographic restriction.
3. Team score is the sum of all team members' scores across the sessions they enter.
4. Team registration deadline: September 4, 2026.

## Log Submission

- Logs must be in **Cabrillo v3** format. Paper logs are not accepted.
- Submit a **separate log for each session**. Do not combine sessions.
- Name logs by call sign plus session number (for example K3XYZ1.log, K3XYZ2.log, K3XYZ3.log).
- Deadline: September 27, 2026.
- Submit at cwops.contesting.com/cwopenlogsubmit.php (upload or paste), then check receipt on the "Logs Received" page on the same site.

Cabrillo QSO format:

    QSO: freq mo date time call nr ex1 call nr ex1
    QSO: 14042 CW 2022-09-03 0000 N5TJ 1 JEFF N6TR 1 TREE

## Approved logging software

CQ/X, CQPWIN, DXLog, GenLog, N1MM, N3FJP, SD, TR4W (N4AF), Writelog, YFKtest and UcxLog.
