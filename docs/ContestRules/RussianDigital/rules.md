## Start / Finish

- **Start:** Saturday of 1st full weekend of October in 12:00 UTC (03 October, 2026 in 12:00)
- **Finish:** Sunday in 11:59 UTC (04 October, 2026 in 11:59)

## Modes

RTTY45, BPSK63

## Bands

160m, 80m, 40m, 20m, 15m, 10m

## Categories (classes)

- SINGLE-OP ALL HIGH
- SINGLE-OP JUNIOR
- MULTI-ONE
- MULTI-ONE JUNIOR
- SINGLE-OP 160M-80M-40M
- SINGLE-OP 20M-15M-10M
- SINGLE-OP ALL QRP
- SINGLE-OP ALL RTTY
- SINGLE-OP ALL BPSK

The output power should not exceed the resolved power according to the radioamateur license of the participant. The outpout
QRP power should not exceed 5 watts.

For all subgroups separate classification for participants from the European part of Russia, participants from the Asian part
of Russia and foreign participants.
For operators under the age of 20, additional separate totals are summed up in the subgroups SINGLE-OP and MULTI-ONE (overall
ranking among all countries of the world). To do this, they must specify JUNIOR in the CATEGORY-OVERLAY line of the log header.
The participants can be declared only one subgroup. In this case, all QSOs must be listed in the log.
SINGLE-OP QRP station: is necessary to indicate in your call /QRP.
MULTI-ONE - Club stations and SES stations: from 1 to 5 operators operators with their personal or SWL callsigns can compile a
team. Personal callsigns should be necessarily specified. Each member of MULTI-ONE team younger than 20 years will be awarded a
certificate for participation in the contest.

The operator may change the bands no more than 10 times within calendar hour (with zero on 59-th minute of each hour).
Only one transmitted signal is permitted at any time.

The repeated contacts are permitted on different bands and different modes providing that a contact will be made not earlier than
in 3 minutes.

## Exchanges

Russian: RST(Q) + 2-letter abbreviation of Russian area (oblast) code (example for Moscow region – 599 MA).
Others: RST(Q) + QSO number, starting 001. The numeration on bands is current.

## Points for QSO

- 1 point per QSO inside the country on 20, 15 and 10 m;
- 2 points per QSO inside the country on 160, 80 and 40 m;
- 3 points per QSO with other country on 20, 15 and 10 m;
- 5 points per QSO with other continents and per QSO with QRP-station on 20, 15 and 10 m;
- 6 points per QSO with other country on 160, 80 and 40 m;
- 10 points per QSO with other continents and per QSO with QRP-station on 160, 80 and 40 m.

## Multipliers

DXCC Countries and Russian areas in each mode on each band.

## Score

The final score is the result of the total points multiplied by the sum of multipliers.

## Logs

Modes in Cabrillo file must be identified as follows:

- RTTY - RY
- BPSK63 - PM

Name your Cabrillo file as your callsign (for example – R3BB.cbr, R3BB.log or R3BB.txt).

## Sending of electronic logs

Log may be upload via Web interface (http://ua9qcq.com/en/submit_log.php?lang=en) or send to e-mail: rusww@rdrclub.ru .

## Deadline

All logs must be sent no later than 5 days after the contest (until 23:59 UTC 7 October 2022).

## Awards

Gallery of prizes and certificates: http://www.rdrclub.ru/gallery-prizes-rdrc .
All certificates for contests are loading on site AWARDS CENTER OF RDRC (http://awards.rdrclub.ru/certificate/).
Awards in electronic type will be awarded to:

1. The certificate of the WINNER:
   - For 1 place on continents in each subgroup, under condition of not less than 3 participants from continent;
   - For 1 place in Asiatic and European Russia in each of subgroups.
2. The certificate of the PRIZEWINNER:
   - For 2 and 3 place on continents in each subgroup, under condition of not less than 5 participants from continent;
   - For 2 and 3 place in Asiatic and European Russia in each of subgroups.
3. The certificate of the PARTICIPANT:
   - To all participants (except for CheckLog) under condition of carrying out not less than 30 CFM QSO.
4. The certificate "Russia map" only to all participants from Russia (except for CheckLog) on Federal districts in each subgroup.
5. Each participant younger than 20 years will be rewarded the personal certificate, for this purpose it is necessary to specify full Name, Surname and date of birth in the log.

Participants who got in DQ will not receive the awards, as well as those who sent reports for check (checklog). Therefore, we
advise you to be necessarily declared in subgroups and not break the rules of our contest.

## Rule Violations, Penalties and Declaration

The contact with correspondent who has not sent the log can be accepted if the callsign of the correspondent can be met not less
than in three different logs.
The Contest Committee will use the mechanism of monitoring of air for the purpose of revealing of the participants who operate
with exceeding of admissible IMD norms, creating considerable QRM and breaking the Rules of the given competitions.

The basis for disqualification can be:

1. The operation with excess of admissible bandwidth of RTTY45 and BPSK63 signal due to overloads, intermodulation, harmonics of a
   sound card and other reasons.
   The Contest Committee will declare the prevention (a yellow card) to the participant for one fixed infringement on quality of
   a signal on the basis of spectrograms-screenshots. The prevention entails the penalty - 30 % of points will be removed in
   results from result of the infringer. Besides for the prevention certificates and prizes will not be given out.
   The Contest Committee will declare DQ (a red card) to participants who will be recorded twice in a time interval not less than
   15 minutes with quality of transmitting signal in BPSK63 on scale Q<7: http://www.rsq-info.net/RSQ-Reporting-Table.html
   and worth, with inadmissible bandwidth of signal in Hz visible on "waterfall" and under condition of presence of a level of air
   noise on the time-and-frequency diagram.
2. The excess of the output power stipulated by given Rules.
3. Transmitted of two and more signals to one moment of time.
4. The use for transmitting of a control number not corresponding to the Rules of contest.
5. Not the message in the cabrillo report of data on a QTH of radio station.
6. Work from territory of Russia the call signs which have been not stipulated by regulating documents of Ministry of
   Communications and GRFC of Russia.
7. More than 30 % of not confirmed QSOs.
8. Unsporting behaviour during contest.

The results of the infringers will be cancelled. The appeals are not accepted!
