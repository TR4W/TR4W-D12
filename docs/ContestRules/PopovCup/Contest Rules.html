Alexander Popov Youth Cup

Start: 2nd Full Weekend Saturday of October in 07:00 UTC (03 October, 2026 in 07:00)
Finish: Saturday in 10:59 UTC (03 October, 2026 in 10:59)
Modes: SSB
Bands:
80m40m20m
Categories (classes):
- SINGLE-OP JUNIOR-19
- SINGLE-OP JUNIOR-25
- MULTI-OP JUNIOR-13
- MULTI-OP JUNIOR-15
- MULTI-OP JUNIOR-19
- MULTI-OP JUNIOR-25
- SINGLE-OP SWL JUNIOR

Separate classification between stations Russian and foreign stations.
SINGLE-OP JR, MULTI-OP JR, SINGLE-OP SWL - yuoth radioamateurs (1997, p. and younger);
SINGLE-OP, MULTI-OP - adult radioamateurs.
In categories MULTI-OP may be 2 or 3 operators.

Teams Competition - the teams of the subjects of the Russian Federation:
The team of the subject of the Russian Federation calculated number of points equal to the sum of the places taken by the
participants of the subject of the Russian Federation, showing the best results in the categories of SINGLE-OP JR and
MULTI-ONE JR. If the command of the subject of the Russian Federation in any one category is not is presented, then it is
calculated the number of points equal to the number of the participants in this category plus one point. The winner is command
of the subject of the Russian Federation, with the minimum number of points.
Competitions are held in 4 rounds, each lasting 60 minutes each.
Maximum number of band changes for MULTI-OP stations should be no more than 30. After 31st band change for MULTI-OP stations
points for QSOs are not charged 
Repeated QSOs are permitted in different time tours, and in the one hour tour on different bands. In any case, between repeated
QSOs on one and the same band should not be less than three minutes.
Exchanges:
Exchanges consist of five digits. The first two digits are age of operator in SINGLE-OP category or age of the oldest participant
of MULTI-ONE radio station. The next three digits indicates the serial number of QSO, since with 001.
Points for QSO:
1 point per QSO.

For SWL:
- 3 points for two-way listening and 1 point for one-way listening;
- listening is a two-way, if fixed both callsignal and both exchanges;
- listening is one-way, if fixed both callsigns signal and one exchange;
- points for each received callsign are considered to be one of the tour at each band;
- at one-way listening to the callsign, not giving points, can meet in one and the same round of not more than five times;
- if a two-way listening to there is an error in one of the exchange, the listening is considered to be one-way;
- repeated listening allowed in the different rounds, and in one round - the different bands.
Score:
The Final result is determined as the sum of QSO points, multiplied by the sum of multipliers.
Logs:
Log is recommended to sent in the form of a file:
- for Russian participants in ERMAK format;
- for foreign participants in Cabrillo format.
Sending of electronic logs:
Log must be upload through Web interface.
Deadline:
13 October, 2022
Awards:
The participants included in the credit awarded with diplomas of "Radio" magazine in electronic form for placing in their
respective categories. 
Teams of subjects of the Russian Federation, took the first three places the team competition among teams of subjects of
the Russian Federation, awarded with diplomas of "Radio" magazine in electronic form.
Foreign participants from among those who sent logs, who took the three best results are awarded with diplomas of "Radio"
magazine in electronic form.
The participants, who took first place in categories SINGLE-OP JR and МULTI-OR JR will be awarded cups of "Radio" magazine.
The certificates in electronic form are sent by email to participants and also posted on the website www.srr.ru.
The participants, who took second and third places in categories SINGLE-OP JR and МULTI-OP JR awarded commemorative plaques
of "Radio" magazine.
For certain categories of participants and their trainers sponsors of sporting events can be established extra prizes.
Rule Violations, Penalties and Declaration:
QSO is not counted for both correspondents:
- if QSO is not confirmed by the log of the correspondent;
- incompatibility exchange, callsign or band in one of the stations;
- when the divergence time of the QSO more than 2 minutes.
Reasons for disqualification
Station can be disqualified: 
- if the number of removed QSOs exceeds 30 percent of their total number. Thus in the number of removed QSOs are not included
QSOs spent with stations who do not send the log;
- if the number of dropped or retransmitted exchanges will exceed five percent of their total number;
- when recorded in the prescribed manner the violations of normative acts regulating the activities of Amateur service in the
Russian Federation (for Russian stations), including, for the use of Amateur radio stations without permits.
