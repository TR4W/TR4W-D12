Atlantic Canada QSO Party -- the 47 county and division multiplier codes (issue 116)

SOURCE OF THE CODES: the sponsor's rules (ACQP-Rules-B-V26.0.1.pdf, V 26.0.1, last updated 2026-05-01)
do NOT list them. They give the counts -- "Nova Scotia (NS- 18), New Brunswick (NB- 15), Prince Edward
Island (PE-3), and Newfoundland (NL-11 Divisions)", 47 -- and say "County/division codes may be found on
the resources page of the contest website" (https://acqp.ca/resources/), which refuses automated fetches.
So the codes are transcribed from DXLog.net's ACQP.txt ("Updated 2026-09-06", its "AC exchanges"
comment lines), whose counts match the sponsor's exactly: NL 11, PE 3, NB 15, NS 18.

SOURCE OF THE NAMES: neither the PDF nor DXLog names them. The names are from the ham2k qso-parties
data (acqp.counties.json, MIT licence, marked "Updated for 2026, but not verified"). Unverified
against the sponsor -- a question on issue 116.

The province is the code's first two letters (the rules: "5-letter province + county/division code").
The sponsor's own Cabrillo example writes "VE1RPX 59 NLKGS"; NLKGS is not in this list (KGS is a
county of PE, NB and NS only), so the example is taken as stale, not as a 48th code.
