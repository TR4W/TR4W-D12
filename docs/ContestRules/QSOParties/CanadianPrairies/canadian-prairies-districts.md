Source: the sponsor's "Federal Electoral District 2023" multiplier table, whose own caption reads
"Total 65 Canadian Prairies District Multipliers". Pasted by NY4I into the conversation on 2026-11-18 (a
screenshot), transcribed by hand: check each abbreviation against the sponsor's page before relying on it.
The rules' prose says "Alberta 34, total 62"; the table gives Alberta 37 and a total of 65, and NY4I
ruled that 65 is the count (2026-11-18). Issue 117.

## Exchanges sent by stations OUTSIDE the prairie provinces

Source: the sponsor's second chart, pasted by NY4I 2026-11-18, captioned "Total 60 Outside Canadian
Prairies Districts Multipliers". The sponsor's note under its heading, quoted:

> Stations within the Canadian prairie provinces who work each other will log 3-letter abbreviations.
> They will receive a provincial multiplier for the first VA/VE4, 5 and 6 station worked on each band,
> including their own province. This will make a total of 66 multipliers for stations within the Canadian
> prairie provinces. Note also that DC counts as MD.

The 60 codes (transcribed from the image; check against the sponsor's page): AL AK AZ AR CA CO CT DE FL GA
HI ID IL IN IA KS KY LA MA ME MD MI MN MS MO MT NE NV NH NJ NM NY NC ND OH OK OR PA RI SC SD TN TX UT VT VA
WA WV WI WY, and the provinces and territories NL NB PE NS QC ON NT NU YT BC. (MD is "Maryland & DC".
The three prairie provinces are not in the list: stations in them send a district.)

**A sponsor inconsistency to settle (issue 117):** the note says an in-area station can earn 66
multipliers, that is, districts plus one province multiplier each for VE4, VE5 and VE6 (3). With 65
districts the total would be 68, not 66; 66 matches the older 63-district table (63 + 3). The class
builds from the 65-district table; the in-area total is not asserted in code.
