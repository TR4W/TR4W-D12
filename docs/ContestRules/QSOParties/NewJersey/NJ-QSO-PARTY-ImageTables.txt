Power categories (verbatim values from the image):
High Power: 150W or more
Low Power: >10W (>5W CW, Dig.) to <150W
QRP: <=10W (SSB), <=5W (CW, Dig.)

Implementation uses the operator's declared CATEGORY-POWER rather than
inferring a category from watts. Factors in the rule text: HIGH 1, LOW 2,
QRP 4. The power table and Cabrillo CATEGORY-POWER retain the distinction
between phone QRP and CW/digital QRP thresholds.
