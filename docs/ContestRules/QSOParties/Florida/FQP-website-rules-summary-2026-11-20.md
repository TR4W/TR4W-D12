# Florida QSO Party rules, from https://floridaqsoparty.org/rules/ (read 2026-11-20)

**This is a restructured SUMMARY of the page, not its verbatim text.** The site refuses scripted
downloads (HTTP 403), so it was read through the assistant's web fetch tool, which does not
reproduce a page verbatim. Numbers, lists and frequency ranges were kept as written. Where this
file and the sponsor's page differ, the page decides. The older text in `FL-QSO-PARTY-Rules.txt`
(fetched 2026-10-26) should be compared with this.

## Contest period
- Starts the last Saturday of April. For 2026: April 25-26.
- Two 10-hour operating periods with a 10-hour break. Operators may work the full 20 hours.
- Saturday 16:00:00Z to Sunday 01:59:59Z; Sunday 12:00:00Z to 21:59:59Z.

## Object
Non-Florida stations work as many Florida stations as possible across the 67 counties. Florida operators can work anyone.

## Categories
- Fixed: Single-Operator (no spotting nets); Single-Operator-Assisted (spotting nets allowed); Multi-Single; Multi-Multi (one signal per band/mode at a time). Single-op categories allow one transmitted signal at a time.
- Mobile (Florida stations only, minimum two counties): Single-Op No Driver; Single-Op with a Driver; Multi-Single; Multi-Multi.
- Portable (Florida stations only): Expedition, a stationary operation from one or more counties; this covers county expeditions. Expeditions and mobiles that move to a new county count as a new station.
- Other: School; Novice/Technician; Special 1x1 (Spelling Bee stations, no multipliers, excluded from club competition); SWL; Check Log.
- Category overlays (noted in Cabrillo): Novice/Technician, SWL, Rookie (licensed under 3 years), YL, Under 25.

## Modes
- Phone, CW, or Mixed. Digital QSOs are not allowed. Cross-mode contacts are not allowed.
- Making a few QSOs on another mode is acceptable and treated as a check log.

## Exchange
- Signal report (RS or RST) and location.
- Florida operators send county. US stations (including KH6/KL7) send state. Canadian stations send province.
- DX stations (including KP4) send DXCC prefix. Maritime mobile stations send ITU Region (1, 2, or 3).

## QSO points
- Phone: 1 point per QSO per band. CW: 2 points per QSO per band. No credit for partial or duplicate contacts.
- Penalty: one additional QSO is deducted for an incorrectly logged callsign or location, or for not being in the other station's log. If that was the only QSO with that location, the multiplier is also lost. Penalty multipliers are not applied.

## Multipliers (counted once per mode, regardless of bands)
- Florida entrants: 50 states (including Florida); District of Columbia; 13 Canadian provinces NS (VE1), NB (VE1, VE9), NL (VO1, VO2), PE (VY2), QC (VE2), ON (VE3), MB (VE4), SK (VE5), AB (VE6), BC (VE7), NT (VE8), NU (VY0), YT (VY1); DXCC countries outside the US and Canada; Maritime Mobile (ITU Regions R1, R2, R3). No in-state county multiplier.
- Non-Florida entrants: 67 Florida counties.

## Power multiplier (applied after QSO points x multipliers)
- 5 watts or less: x3. More than 5 watts, under 100 watts: x2. More than 100 watts: no multiplier. If no power is indicated, High Power is assumed.

## Final score
- (QSO points x multipliers) x power multiplier.
- Mobile and Expedition scores use cumulative QSOs and multipliers, counted once overall.
- Special 1x1 stations have no multiplier.

## Suggested frequencies
- CW: 7.025-7.055, 14.025-14.055, 21.025-21.055, 28.025-28.055 MHz. Phone: 7.185-7.285, 14.225-14.325, 21.300-21.400, 28.400-28.500 MHz.
- No 160 m, 80 m, WARC, or VHF.
- CW mobile windows: 7.025-7.035, 14.040-14.050, 21.040-21.050, 28.040-28.050 MHz. Fixed and Expedition stations should not call CQ or run stations there.

## Miscellaneous
- Remote receiver, transmitter, or antenna use is allowed; the location is where the equipment is. Multiple remote sites may be used, but not at the same time. Separate receiving sites are not allowed.
- Florida stations must send their county. Do not log "FL" as the exchange.
- Each station may be worked once per mode per band, up to 8 QSOs total. Mobiles and Expeditions may be worked in each county they operate.
- No limits on band changes.
- Club competition: put "CLUB:" in the Cabrillo header. The club total is the sum of QSOs, not scores. Mobile and Expedition entries count for Florida clubs. Special 1x1 entries do not. A club needs more than one entry to be listed. Multi-op QSO splits by club are allowed.
- No self-spotting or requesting spots, except for Special 1x1 stations.
- Contacts using second callsigns earn no credit.

## Reporting
- Electronic Cabrillo logs are encouraged. Each QSO needs: band, mode, date, UTC time, sent callsign, sent RST and location, received callsign, received RST and location.
- Upload page (preferred): fqp.contesting.com/fqpsubmitlog.php. Email: logs@floridaqsoparty.org. Web form for paper logs: b4h.net/cabforms/flqp_cab3.php.
- Deadline: 10 days after the contest ends.
