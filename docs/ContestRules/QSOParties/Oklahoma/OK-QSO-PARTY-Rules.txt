                         SUBMIT LOGS BY APRIL 5

· Objective: For Amateurs outside of Oklahoma to make contact with as many Oklahoma
    stations as possible. For Oklahoma Amateurs to activate as many Oklahoma counties as
    possible and work everyone.

· Period of Operation: Everyone may operate the entire 19 hour period.
· Bands: Operate only the 3.5, 7, 14, 21, 28, 50, and 144 MHz bands.

             Suggested frequencies for CW - 3541, 7041, 14041, 21041, 28041
              Phone - plus or minus 3.805, 7.195, 14.260, 21.335, 28.470, 50.130 MHz.
             If your are a Mobile station, please CQ (on CW) below the suggested frequencies, if
             your a Fixed station please CQ (on CW) above the suggested frequencies.
· Modes: Operators may operate the following. (Fixed Multi op must enter HIGH MIXED)
             Mixed mode (Phone, CW, Digital (NO FT8 allowed).
             Phone only.
             CW only.
· Exchange:
             Oklahoma stations send signal report and county.
             W/VE stations (including KH6/KL7) send signal report and state or province.
             DX stations (including KH2/KP4) send signal report and DXCC prefix.
· QSO Points:
             Oklahoma stations work anyone
             Count two points per phone QSO with any station.
             Count three points per CW QSO.
             Count three points per Digital QSO (No FT8/FT4)
             Stations may be worked again on each band and mode.
             Non Oklahoma stations work only Oklahoma stations
             Count two points per phone QSO with any OK station.
             Count three points per CW QSO with any OK station
             Count three points per Digital QSO (No FT8/FT4)
             Stations may be worked again on each band and mode.
· Multipliers:
             Oklahoma stations
             The 50 states (DC counts as Maryland); The 13 Canadian Provinces/Territories - NS,
             NB, NL, PE, QC, ON, MB, SK, AB, BC, NT, NU, YT ; The 77 Oklahoma counties;
             DXCC countries* (excluding US, Canada, KH6 and KL7). A Multiplier counts once,
             regardless of the number bands or modes it is worked on. *(note: OK stations please do
             not abuse the unlimited dxcc mult rule by s/p only DX stations).
             Non Oklahoma stations
             The 77 Oklahoma counties.

             Oklahoma mobile stations that change counties are considered to be a new station and
             may be worked again for QSO and multiplier credit. Oklahoma mobile stations
             operating on a 2, 3, or 4 county line may be counted as 2, 3, or 4 QSO and
             multipliers. Separate log entries must be entered for each county/QSO by both
             Oklahoma and Non Oklahoma stations. Make sure you can get to the County line.
             Operate close by if it is not safe to be on the line. Operating from either end of a
             bridge on a water county line is OK. Do not operate in a unsafe spot. Do not park
             in the middle of a bridge. Note that County hunters may require different criterion
             than the OKQP. A multiplier counts once, regardless of the number of bands or modes
             it is worked on.
·
· Categories:
             Oklahoma - Fixed station

                      SOHP - Single operator high power (over 100 watts)
                      SOLP - Single operator low power ( less than 100 watts)
                      QRP - Single operator QRP (5 watts or less)
                      Multi-operator - any number of operators. No power limits, mixed mode only

                          Multi 1 - One transmitted signal.
                          Multi Multi - Any number of transmitted signals. (only one per band/mode)

Oklahoma Mobile
        OKLAHOMA MOBILE NON-ASSISTED - Oklahoma Mobile No driver or
        second op. CW only, SSB only, or Mixed allowed. (100 watts max)
        OKLAMOMA MOBILE ASSISTED - Oklahoma Mobile with a driver. Driver
        can NOT operate (100 watts max). There can be more than two people in the
        Mobile, but they may not operate or assist the operator or driver at anytime. This
        means they can not be the navigator. If you want another operator or navigator
        please enter as Mobile Unlimited. The driver may use a different call sign to
        operate.
        OKLAHOMA MOBILE UNLIMITED - Oklahoma Mobile Unlimited - any
        number of operators or drivers. No power limit (other than legal). Only one
        transmitted signal at a time. Mixed mode only.

· Scoring:
             Multiply total QSO Points by total Multipliers.

· Bonus

             Oklahoma Mobile stations can earn 500 points per county by making at least 10 QSO in
             the county.
             -
· Miscellaneous:
             Spotting nets, packet node, internet spotting websites etc, can be used by all stations.
             Contacts through repeaters, digipeaters, internet, or gateways are not permitted.
             Pack Roving / California train operation is absolutely not allowed. Two mobiles
             traveling together may work each other one time from each county combination (for
             example only on 40 meters or only on 20 meters not all bands). This does not preclude
             mobiles that happen to cross paths by chance from working each other on all bands. Do
             not put PIT/LAT/HAS on the same line in your log. Use a separate line for each county.
             No FT8/FT4. No repeaters.

             Please exhibit good sportsmanship and distribute your contacts among the various
             mobiles. "Cheerleading", defined as working only one mobile as they change counties,
             is highly discouraged.
