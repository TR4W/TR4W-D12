Scope/Rules/Eligibility

This document outlines the rules for the Missouri QSO Party, sponsored by the Boeing
Employees Amateur Radio Society-St. Louis (BEARS-St. Louis). The General Rules for All
ARRL contests will apply except as noted. Any licensed radio amateur may enter.

Objective

The objective of the contest is to work as many stations as possible during the contest period, to
provide all licensed radio amateurs outside the state of Missouri the opportunity to work
Missouri counties and to provide all licensed radio amateurs in Missouri an opportunity to work
other states and countries. Missouri stations should call CQ Missouri QSO Party or CQ MOQP.
Stations calling Missouri should call CQ Missouri or CQ MO.

Date and Time Period

The contest has two separate operating periods. For 2026 due to the Easter weekend the
contest is on 11-12th of April.

Day      Begin              End
1st Day  1400 UTC Saturday  0400 UTC Sunday
2nd Day  1400 UTC Sunday    2000 UTC Sunday

Modes and Frequencies

CW, Digital, and phone operation on 160M, 80M, 40M, 20M, 15M, 10M, 6M, 2M, 1.25M, and
70cm. Suggested frequencies are 1.820 MHz on 160M or 40 kHz up from the band edge on
CW, and 1.880, 3.825, 7.220, 14.250, 21.380, and 28.350 MHz on SSB.. Suggested
frequencies on VHF/UHF are the standard calling frequencies.

Exchange

Missouri Stations
Missouri stations will provide call sign, RST, and a three letter county code as defined in Table 1.
Non-Missouri Stations
Non-Missouri stations will provide call sign, RST, and their US state (as defined in Table 2) or
Canadian province or territory (as defined in Table 3).

DX Stations
DX stations will provide callsign, RST, and the exchange "DX".

Score

The score for a participating station will be determined by multiplying the total number of valid
contact points by the total number of multipliers worked, then adding the bonus points (if
applicable).

Points
Points for valid contacts will be awarded in the following manner:

    1. Valid phone contacts equals one point each.
    2. Valid CW contacts equals two points each.
    3. Valid digital contacts equals two points each.
    4. At least one valid contact with the WØMA special event station will count as a single

         100-point bonus added to the total score.
    5. At least one valid contact with special event station KØGQ will count as a single

         100-point bonus added to the total score.
    6. A 100 point bonus will be awarded for successfully submitting a Cabrillo log

         electronically.
    7. Any valid contacts made between the hours of 1400 UTC Saturday to 2000 UTC

         Saturday and 1400 UTC Sunday to 2000 UTC Sunday on the 40 and 80 meter bands
         will count as an additional point each. Up to 250-point bonus can be added to the total
         score.

Multipliers
Multipliers will be awarded in the following manner:

    1. The multipliers for Missouri stations are Missouri counties (115 maximum), US states (49
         maximum), and Canadian provinces and territories (13 maximum) worked. An additional
         multiplier of the value of one will be added if at least one DX station is worked.

    2. The multipliers for Non-Missouri and DX stations are Missouri Counties (115 maximum)
         worked.

    3. Any mobile or portable category entry that makes 50 or more valid contacts from a
         county or county lines will be given the multiplier for that county or counties.
Entry Classifications

All Missouri stations may work any other Missouri station once per mode on each band per
Missouri county. Missouri stations may work non-Missouri stations once per mode per band. All
non-Missouri stations may work Missouri stations once per mode on each band per Missouri
county. MOQP participants may not submit entries in multiple categories. Single operator
stations may only have one transmitted signal at a time.

Missouri Fixed Stations
Missouri Fixed stations will operate from a single county within the State of Missouri. Fixed
stations may operate in one of the following categories:

         Missouri Fixed Multi-Op - any legal power, multi/multi allowed
         Missouri Fixed Single-Op, High Power - over 150W, one transmitted signal
         Missouri Fixed Single-Op, Low Power - 150W or less, one transmitted signal
         Missouri Fixed Single-Op, QRP - 5W or less, one transmitted signal

Missouri Expedition
Missouri Expedition stations will operate from a single fixed geographic location. Commercial
power may be used. The station and all antennas must be temporarily erected for the contest
and removed afterwards. The location may not be that of a permanent amateur station.

Expedition stations may be located at the intersection of two or more counties. County line
operations must log these contacts as separate QSOs. Single-op expedition operations from
county lines must have a portion of the station physically located inside each county claimed.
Multi-op expedition operations from county lines must have a portion of at least one station
physically located inside each county claimed. All equipment (including antennas) must lie
within a circle whose diameter does not exceed 300 meters (1000 feet).

Expedition stations may operate from one of the following categories:

         Missouri Expedition Multi-Op - any legal power, multi/multi allowed
         Missouri Expedition Single-Op, High Power - over 150W
         Missouri Expedition Single-Op, Low Power - 150W or less
         Missouri Expedition Single-Op, QRP - 5W or less

Missouri Mobile Stations
Mobile stations must operate from at least two counties within the State of Missouri and may
operate in motion. Mobile operation on county lines is permitted. When operating from a county
line, each contact must be logged as a separate QSO for each county claimed.
Mobile entries with one operator will be classified as Single-Op. Mobile entries with two or more
operators, regardless of driving responsibilities, will be classified as Multi-Op.
Mobile stations may operate from one of the following categories:

         Missouri Mobile Unlimited - over 150W multi/multi allowed
         Missouri Mobile Multi-Op, Low Power - 150W or less,
         Missouri Mobile Single-Op, Low Power, Mixed - 150W or less
         Missouri Mobile Single-Op, Low Power, CW - 150W or less
         Missouri Mobile Single-Op, Low Power, Phone - 150W or less

Missouri Portable Stations
Portable stations must operate from at least two counties within the State of Missouri and may
not operate while in motion. Portable operation on county lines is permitted. County line
operations must log all contacts as separate QSOs.

Portable stations may operate from one of the following categories:

         Missouri Portable Unlimited - over 150W multi/multi allowed
         Missouri Portable Multi-Op, Low Power - 150W or less,
         Missouri Portable Single-Op, Low Power, Mixed - 150W or less
         Missouri Portable Single-Op, Low Power, CW - 150W or less
         Missouri Portable Single-Op, Low Power, Phone - 150W or less

Non-Missouri Stations
Non-Missouri stations are from any other state in the United States (including the District of
Columbia (DC), Alaska and Hawaii), or any Canadian province or territory.

         Non-Missouri US Single Operator High Power - Over 150W
         Non-Missouri US Single Operator Low Power - 150W or less
         Non-Missouri US Single Operator QRP - 5W or less
         Non-Missouri Multi Operator - Any legal power, multi/multi allowed
         Canada - Any legal power, multi/multi allowed

DX Stations
DX stations are from any entity on the ARRL DXCC Countries list excluding the United States,
Canada, Alaska, and Hawaii. DX stations can operate any legal power. Single op or Multi Op is
allowed.

Missouri School Club Category
Missouri School clubs entries can be any university, college, technical, primary, or secondary
school. Only currently enrolled students and faculty/staff of the institution are eligible to operate
a school club entry. Alumni may participate on the air with students, however it is not in the spirit
of the category for alumni exclusively to use the club station. Alumni should make every effort to
continue in an Elmer role to current student club members. If no on campus station exists, a
club may operate from a members station or set up a portable station on campus.

Missouri Rookie Category

All Missouri amateurs who are 18 years old or younger or have been licensed less than 3 years
are eligible to enter in the rookie category in addition to the categories defined above. Rookie
entries should be noted as such in the Cabrillo file header.

Missouri Club Category

Missouri stations may specify a club affiliation with their entry. Club scores will be determined by
adding the scores of all Missouri stations that specify a particular club in their entry. At least
three logs must be submitted to constitute a valid club entry. A club entry form is not required.

Miscellaneous Rules

The following miscellaneous rules will apply:

    1. As a condition of entry, participants are bound to the provisions and intent of these rules,
         and bound to the decisions of the contest sponsor regarding award distribution.

    2. The use of spotting assistance or nets (operating arrangements involving other
         individuals, DX-alerting nets, packet clusters, etc.) not physically located at the station is
         permitted. Stations may spot themselves. Please see the BEARS website for APRS
         tracking, spotting and the MOQP Facebook page links.

    3. Cross-band and cross-mode contacts will not be permitted.
    4. Repeaters and gateways may not be used to complete QSOs.
    5. Soliciting a simplex contact during the contest period is permitted. APRS or other similar

         means may be used for posting location, frequency, and mode but may not be used for
         conducting QSOs.
    6. Satellite contacts are prohibited.
    7. Any digital mode contact counts as a valid QSO for digital. You can only work the same
         digital station once per band. (The intent of this rule is to prevent working the same
         station on a band via multiple digital modes.)
    8. FT4/FT8 or similar modes that do not allow free text sending will not be allowed.
    9. All stations should provide the opportunity to be worked by everyone involved in the
         contest. Stations whose operation appears to be providing QSOs or multipliers only to a
         specific station or stations is considered unsportsmanlike conduct and may be
         disqualified.
    10.Should more than one station attain the same score, the station with the most Missouri
         counties worked will be awarded the higher position. The highest number of MO counties
         plaques and certificates will be awarded to the station with the earliest QSO time of their
         last county worked.
Special Event Stations

The BEARS-St. Louis will operate a special event station, WØMA as part of the contest. A
one-time (first QSO only) 100-point bonus will be added to the total score of stations working
special event station WØMA. WØMA is normally located in St. Louis County (SLC), but the
station may operate portable in a different Missouri county for the QSO party.

The Raytown ARC will operate a second event station, KØGQ as part of the contest. A one-time
(first QSO only) 100-point bonus will be added to the total score of stations working special
event station KØGQ. KØGQ will be operated out of Jackson County (JAC).

Reporting, Submissions & Results

Entries must be submitted to the contest sponsor within 15 days after the conclusion of the
contest. No late entries will be accepted. All logs generated electronically, must be submitted
electronically in Cabrillo format. No paper logs will be accepted. Entry can be found at:
https://www.w0ma.org/index.php/missouri-qso-party. Official results will be published on the
BEARS-St. Louis web site approximately 90 days after entry submittal deadline.

All entries must specify their entry class and power level in the Cabrillo file header or summary
sheet. (Note that some logging software does not correctly populate this information. Please
verify the accuracy of your Cabrillo header using a text editor before submission). Incomplete or
illegible entries will be classified as check-logs. Non-Cabrillo entries without a summary sheet
will be scored as check-logs.

E-mail Entry
E-mail entries to: moqsoparty@w0ma.org

Bonus Missouri QSO Party Certificates

Awarded to stations who spell SHOWME or MISSOURI using the suffixes of selected 1x1 calls
listed below. For letters that occur more than once, the 1x1 stations may be reused if worked on
additional bands or modes. Special Event Stations WØMA and KØGQ count as wild cards and
may be used in place of one missing letter.

Example: NØS WØH KØO NØW KØM NØE or NØS WØH WØMA NØW KØM NØE

The following 1x1 call signs count towards the bonus Awards: KØE, KØH, KØI, KØM, KØO,
KØR, KØS, KØU, KØW, NØE, NØH, NØI, NØM, NØO, NØR, NØS, NØU, NØW, WØE, WØH,
WØI, WØM, WØO, WØR, WØS, WØU, and WØW.

All bonus certificate winners will be notified via email that their certificate is available to be
downloaded from www.w0ma.org

Digital awards

The digital mode plaque and certificates will be awarded to stations with the most digital QSOs
multiplied by the most multipliers worked digitally. Entries do not have to be digital only entries
and logs may also include non-digital contacts.

VHF awards

The VHF certificates will be awarded to stations with the most VHF QSOs multiplied by the most
multipliers worked on VHF. Entries do not have to be VHF only entries and logs may also
include non-VHF contacts.
