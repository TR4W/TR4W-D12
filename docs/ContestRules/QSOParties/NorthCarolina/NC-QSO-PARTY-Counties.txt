Source: https://ncqsoparty.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/NCQP_Abbreviations_221121.pdf
Fetched: 2026-11-20

The abbreviation is the capital letters of each name (DaViDson = DVD).
The sponsor spells CHO "CHOwen", which is no North Carolina county; Chowan is
presumably meant. Not confirmed, so nc_cty.dom leaves CHO unnamed.
