# Hawaii QSO Party (HIQP) - sponsor rules

Source: hawaiiqsoparty.org (rules page and multiplier map). The site refuses automated fetches (403),
so NY4I pasted this text into issue 118 on 2026-11-17. Sponsor: https://www.hawaiiqsoparty.org
Log submission: hqp.contesting.com. This is the text as pasted; the dates are quoted as the sponsor
wrote them and are checked against the calendar below.

1. Operating period is 36 hours from 1600 UTC Aug 21 through 0359 UTC Aug 23, 2026.
   Note: that is 6am Saturday Aug 21 to start and end at 6pm Sunday in Hawaii.
2. Stations must be worked only on the 10, 15, 20, 40, 80, and 160 meter bands.
3. Stations can be worked only once per band-mode (CW, SSB, digital).
4. Digital mode includes ALL digital modes (RTTY, PSK, QPSK, FT-x, VarAC, etc) as "digital"
   (https://www.hawaiiqsoparty.org/digital-definition/).
5. Use of spotting networks is permitted and encouraged.
6. Operation must conform to home country regulations.
7. Logs must be submitted electronically no later than 14 days following the event.
8. Exchanges
   - Hawai'i stations send signal report plus a Hawai'i multiplier (district).
   - Non-Hawai'i stations send signal report plus State or Province as appropriate.
   - Non-USA / Canada stations send "DX".
   - NOTE: if your mode cannot send the proper QTH name, then a Grid Square (4 characters) is allowed
     (https://www.hawaiiqsoparty.org/grid-squares/).

## Points

SSB: 2 points. CW: 3 points. Digital: 3 points.

## Multipliers

- Non-Hawaiian stations: the 14 Hawai'i districts per band. Maximum of 84 (6 bands x 14 districts).
- Hawai'i stations: the 14 Hawai'i districts plus US states (including DC), plus Canadian provinces,
  plus DXCC entities. ONCE ONLY - NOT PER BAND.
- Grid squares are allowed if a multiplier name is not possible for the mode (for example FT8). Log
  checking will determine the most likely multiplier and apply it to the final score.

## Final score

Total QSO points times the number of earned multipliers.

## The 14 Hawai'i districts (DXLog's list; the sponsor's map is an image in the issue)

KAU Kauai, NII Niihau, HON Honolulu, LHN Leeward, PRL Pearl Harbor Area (DXLog spells it "Perl"),
WHN Windward, KAL Kalawao, LNI Lana`i, MAU Maui, MOL Molokai, HIL Hilo, KOH Kohala, KON Kona,
VOL Volcano Park.

## Contacts

Each station can be worked three times per band. Once each on CW, SSB, digital (digital is any
binary mode including RTTY, FT8, FT4, Winlink P2P, etc.). (NY4I, 2026-11-17, the sponsor's rule.)

## Grid squares

(Sponsor text, pasted by NY4I into issue 118, 2026-11-17, as summarised in his ruling.) A digital
QSO (FT8, FT4 and the like, which cannot send a QTH name) may log the received 4-character grid
square instead of a district, state, province or DX. If no grid was received the operator logs a
fake one such as ZZ00; a blank received QTH scores zero. Any 4-character grid is accepted. The
multiplier for a grid QSO is determined by the sponsor from the callsign's mailing address. The
sponsor's Cabrillo example writes the grid in the received QTH column:

    QSO: 14080 DG 2020-08-22 0528 KH6TU -04 MAU W6OAT -10 CN87

Your own transmitted QTH is never a grid: it is always a district, state, province or DX.

## Homepage notes

(Sponsor text from the hawaiiqsoparty.org homepage, pasted by NY4I on 2026-11-17.)

- HI stations may move from one district to another if they identify as being in that district.
  AH7RF/KOH is a different station than AH7RF/KON and can be worked again on the same band and
  mode. Such mobile stations must submit a separate log for each activation.
- HI stations may activate multiple districts by sitting on the boundary with some part of their
  operation in each. They must identify each such as AH7RF/KOH/KON. Log such contacts as TWO
  contacts, one from each location. Each is scored separately and not considered a duplicate.
- Other contests (WW Digi, OH and KS QSO parties) overlap; a combined log is accepted if it has at
  least one Hawaiian QSO. The Cabrillo header must carry CONTEST: HI-QSO-PARTY.
- Self-spotting is not allowed.

## Open points (resolved)

- DATES -- RESOLVED (NY4I, 2026-11-17): the pasted "1600 UTC Aug 21 through 0359 UTC Aug 23, 2026"
  is a SPONSOR TYPO (Aug 21 2026 is a Friday). The event starts on the Saturday: 2026 is Sat Aug 22
  1600Z to Mon Aug 24 0400Z (the calendar row), 2027 is Sat Aug 21 (the sponsor's date), 36 hours
  each. Both are the Saturday before the 4th Sunday of August; no "Nth full weekend" rule gives both.
  The class states `the Saturday before the 4th Sunday of August at 16:00 UTC for 36h` (the grammar
  form NY4I approved on 2026-11-17).
