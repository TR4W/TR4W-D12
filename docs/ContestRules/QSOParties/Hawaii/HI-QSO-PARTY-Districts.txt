Hawaii QSO Party multiplier districts, transcribed 2026-11-17
Source: the sponsor's multiplier map (hawaiiqsoparty.org/mult-map/, an image NY4I pasted into issue 118) and DXLog.net HIQP.txt / HIQPHI.txt [MULTIPLIERS] (updated 2026-09-05).

The districts are NOT counties: ADIF's Hawaii counties are Hawaii, Honolulu, Kalawao, Kauai and Maui.
